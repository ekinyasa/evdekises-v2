# EVDEKİ SES
## Antigravity — Landing Experience Build Brief

You are the lead digital art director, conversion-experience designer and frontend implementer for this task.

Your job is NOT to produce a design proposal.

Your job is to DESIGN AND BUILD a high-fidelity, responsive, browser-renderable landing page for the first paid test cohort of EVDEKİ SES by Nilüfer Ormanlı.

This page has two real jobs:

1. Nilüfer must be able to review and approve it as a representation of her work.
2. A suitable visitor must be able to understand the program, decide whether it is for her, and proceed toward purchasing the cohort.

This is therefore neither:
- an art experiment
nor
- a generic sales funnel.

It must succeed as both a designed digital experience and a conversion landing page.

# 2. THE PRODUCT

EVDEKİ SES is a six-week live group program for women who have been carrying something they want to bring into the world.

The participant chooses one real thing and works with it through the program until it has a first complete/shareable form and encounters another person/the world before the program ends.

The process involves:
voice,
body,
deep listening,
creative material,
decisions,
form,
live relational work,
partner work,
daily/weekly practices,
and actual work on the participant's chosen material.

The program is NOT primarily:
- singing training
- a music course
- a generic creativity course
- therapy
- women's wellness
- spiritual lifestyle coaching
- business coaching
- productivity coaching

Voice is part of the method and the field of inquiry.

The actual movement is from something carried internally toward form and encounter.

Understand this deeply before designing.

Do not turn this paragraph into public copy.

# 4. CRITICAL ART-DIRECTION PROBLEM

A long source text does NOT mean the website should look like a long document.

Do not produce:

- a designed PDF
- a magazine article
- an old-school sales letter
- a blog post
- a syllabus
- a course-platform page
- a stack of alternating colored sections
- endless heading + paragraph blocks
- a collection of SaaS cards

The browser experience must be authored spatially.

Think in VIEWPORT-SCALE SCENES.

SCENE
→ transition
→ SCENE
→ transition
→ SCENE

The visitor should repeatedly encounter a new visual mode of understanding the product.

Some scenes may be photographic.
Some may be quiet.
Some may be dark.
Some may be light.
Some may be information-dense.
Some may use a controlled sticky/layered relationship.
Some may simply use exceptional typography and space.

But they must not all share the same composition.
